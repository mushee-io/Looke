# M19 — Community participation and provisional rewards accounting

**Status: partial source. No funded reward scheme, token balances, point redemption, airdrop entitlement, claim contract, staking, withdrawal or distribution exists.**

M19 adds a transparent, non-redeemable **offchain participation score** based on real Looke social rows. This is an activity/engagement measure for potential operator review, **not** proof of an independent human, proof of organic community growth, a creator compensation contract, or an approved reward allocation.

## Live, source-derived scoring

- **Policy v1:** review the current rolling **14 UTC calendar days** including today. Only visible posts and engagement with visible posts count; comments must themselves be visible. Self-comment, self-like and self-repost do not score. Undone likes/unfollows and removed or hidden content disappear from the recalculated live ledger.
- **Crosspost deduplication:** for all engagement types, only one action per **other creator, type and UTC day** qualifies. This limits repeated likes/comments/reposts on one author's posts, but does not stop an adversary controlling multiple creator accounts. Authored posts are counted by distinct post ID.
- **Daily category caps:** at most 2 visible posts × 8 points; 3 unique-target comments × 3; 4 unique-target likes × 1; 3 unique-target reposts × 2; and 2 unique-target follows × 1. **Maximum 37 provisional points/day**, 518 over 14 days, with independently visible discarded-over-cap counts in the private activity breakdown. There are no automatic bonus events, referral points or trading-volume incentives.
- **Review prerequisites:** profile age >=7 elapsed days, at least 3 UTC days with qualifying activity, and interaction with at least 2 different other creators across the period. Meeting these checks returns `qualifiesForManualReview`; it **does not** mark the account as approved or distribute any rewards. **These gates are not proof against Sybil farming.** Operators still need independent risk controls and review.
- **Moderation protections:** current `moderation_status='visible'` on posts and comments controls scoring. Unreviewed spam/abuse *reports* are not automatic penalties—adversaries could otherwise use false reports to suppress competitors. Moderators must have audited, documented review procedures before any financial program.
- **Consistency:** `lib/server/community-rewards.ts` computes source-derived counts in one PostgreSQL **repeatable-read, read-only transaction** for each signed-in creator. Activity is grouped into days, and distinct counterpart creators are counted. Inputs and policy arithmetic are validated in `lib/community-rewards.mjs` and regression tests.
- **Private live API:** `GET /api/community/rewards` requires Looke's valid signed EVM session, limits data to the **current account**, and returns a daily category breakdown, cap effects, the policy, review prerequisites, and up to seven recent unverified snapshots. No public wallet leaderboard or individual action IDs are exposed.
- **Aggregate community API:** `GET /api/community/stats` returns only counts of currently visible posts, distinct authors with visible posts and currently visible comments in the last 14 days. It never publishes per-person scores, wallet identifiers, vote tallies or sybil risk assessments. These are not verified unique humans.
- **User-triggered provisional snapshots:** `POST /api/community/rewards/snapshot` requires the signed-in account and same-origin request and stores an **offchain checkpoint of the last 14 fully completed UTC days**, not the live partial day. It captures per-day capped scores, a summary and the SHA-256 of their aggregate JSON in `looke_participation_snapshots`. Unchanged requests are idempotent, changed data (e.g. later moderation) increments a revision and resets review status to `unverified`. **The digest commits only to aggregate observations, not a cryptographic proof of each underlying event.** It is neither an operator audit nor a payout ledger.
- **Staging migration:** `db/014_participation_snapshots.sql` creates Looke's dedicated profile-owned snapshot table with unique (profile, period_start, policy_version), strict score/day ranges, summary-digest constraints, and `review_status='unverified'` enforced as a database check. Applied only to isolated Looke Neon staging; validated schema and scoring SQL. There is deliberately **no credited balance, token amount or claim address** in the table.
- **Creator UI:** `components/CommunityRewards.tsx` appears under Profile and Activity, displaying public high-level activity, private provisional scores and activity caps, manual-review prerequisites and historical unverified checkpoints. No example activity or wallet earnings are inserted as real metrics.

## Risk boundaries and limitations

- **Public wallet sessions are not unique humans:** coordination between wallets, self-owned counterpart accounts, follow rings, purchased likes and spam are still possible. Rate caps, cross-author uniqueness and age gates are only entry-level friction; they do not establish economic-grade Sybil resistance.
- A current source-derived score is **mutable**: removal/moderation/unlikes/reposts can change the result. Historical snapshots may become outdated until a new user-initiated reconciliation. They are not final transaction books, and do not preserve underlying historical actions once deleted.
- There is no operator interface to clear or suspend a reward claim, no independently trusted personhood, no independent event archive, no budget, tokenomics or reward contract. The database deliberately constrains every saved review to `unverified` and all user-visible scores to non-redeemable.
- A badge or review prerequisite **must not** be represented as a guaranteed grant, transferable token, income, revenue, retrodrop allocation or realized creator earning. M17 royalty evidence remains separately unverified, and M18 onchain activity is not included in community scoring.
- Protect query endpoints from scraping/abuse (WAF, rate limits, rate-limit dashboards and bot behavior metrics); test privacy leakage, heavy activity and concurrent snapshot requests. The snapshot route is user-triggered and authenticated, but broad abuse detection and operator moderation remain outstanding.
- Scoring does not use payments, swaps or market volume, preventing fake testnet turnover from manufacturing reward entitlement. All production token rewards are disabled.

## Required acceptance before a reward/retrodrop launch

1. Fix the existing Vercel deployment permission issue, deploy the protected Looke staging environment connected only to isolated Neon and test actual session/Origin checks, SQL performance and multi-account privacy.
2. Independently audit reward formulas, costs, scoring version changes, deletion/moderation handling, database concurrency, timestamp boundaries and replay/identity attacks. Build dedicated query indexes and WAF rate caps as needed after realistic load tests.
3. Design anti-Sybil defenses beyond wallet age and activity thresholds: controlled reputation/identity, linked-account signals with privacy/consent, follow-farm graphs, hard account review limits, flagged-content review and appeals; avoid unfair treatment of new users.
4. Build a tamper-evident **event provenance ledger** of moderated actions and source evidence, a protected operator re-evaluation queue, dispute/appeal workflows, explicit final snapshot seals and independent reviewer signoff. Current aggregate SHA-256 snapshots are not sufficiently auditable for a financial reward claim.
5. Establish token supply, permitted claimants, allocation methodology, funding, wallet associations, chain choice, taxes/compliance, user agreements, spend limits and treasury governance. Independent audited token distribution/claim contracts and anti-replay signatures must precede any real payout.
6. Verify a genuine signed Ultra testnet distribution to diverse wallets with receipts, reconciliation, failed-claim recovery and account ownership proofs; do not infer that activity implies earned tokens.
7. Obtain legal/security approval, accessibility and production monitoring acceptance before a public reward campaign.

**M19 is source-implemented but operationally partial.** No users have received a token or a redeemable reward from this work.
